FRONTLINE HIGH SCHOOL WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: all website sections and editable text
- styles.css: responsive styling for mobile, tablet and desktop
- script.js: mobile navigation and footer year

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts, the map embed and the illustrative online banner/about images.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace [Add Name], [Student Name], [CLASS], [DATE], [Add founding year] and other bracketed text with school-approved details.
- Replace all photo placeholder panels with photos approved by Frontline High School.
- The banner/about images currently use illustrative stock photos, not verified photos of this school.
- Confirm school hours, grades, board, admission details, and founding year with the school office.
- The admissions enquiry button opens WhatsApp to +91 98859 73220.
- Contact phone and address are based on the details supplied / public business listing.

The site is static and can be hosted on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel or any static web host.
